What it does

Built to guide, not to distract

Computer visionCOCO-SSD finds people, vehicles and furniture, with distance and approach tracking.
Depth AIDepth Anything spots walls and unknown obstacles that object detection misses.
Audio guidanceShort spoken alerts such as "Chair ahead. Move slightly left."
Haptic alertsA gentle tap, fast pulses or one long buzz, depending on how close and how risky.
Voice commandsSay start, stop or repeat. No need to touch the screen.
Three languagesEnglish, हिन्दी and ಕನ್ನಡ, for alerts and commands.
Stairs and drop-offsThe depth map flags sudden steps in the floor ahead, with an early "Stop".
Low-light warningTells you when the picture is too dark for detection to be reliable.
SOS and test logSend your location by WhatsApp, and export every alert as a CSV file.
